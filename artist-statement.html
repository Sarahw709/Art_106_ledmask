Artist Statement

My piece titled “I See You, You See Me” is a half-mask made of paper and Led strips that resembles a traditional Japanese Kitsune mask. While one eye functions as a normal eye hole to peer out from behind the mask, the other eye showcases two written messages. Using a paper slider technique, the paper slider can be moved up and down to expose the words “I see you” and “you see me”. The mask consists of vibrant shades of red, yellow, and orange, with red and yellow Led lights also showing on one half of the mask.

I was inspired by the traditional Japanese Kitsune mask because it is believed to represent intelligence and protection. Additionally, the traditional Kitsune fox being portrayed serves as a symbol of concealed identity. The two messages on the left eye, “I see you” and “you see me” are meant to represent the shifting perspective of being watched/surveilled vs. being the one watching/surveilling. Additionally, the Leds only occupying one half of the mask contribute to the two contrasting points of view. Using symbolism, vibrant colors, and alternating messages, this mask serves as an exploration of shifting perspectives between the surveillor and the surveilled.
